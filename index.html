About

Who I am

My name is Roman Patlai. I build software that automates the routine — from outreach platforms to trading bots.

Apart from my love of building, I also love to gain knowledge. Unknown things excite me. Once I have gained knowledge, I think it is almost an obligation to use it. I also believe that knowledge alone is not enough. I have developed a value system called Beyondia. If you want to know which philosophy I follow and what I believe in, you can read about it on the Beyond Website.

I want to be sure that I provide a positive surplus to the company I work for. I love to concentrate on the things that really matter and optimize them. I’m a very well-organized individual — it contributes to the high predictability of my work and its results.

    I love the details no one notices, but everyone feels.

  • Honesty

Beyondia

A country of ideas

Beyondia is an idea that holds that people are made close by the ideas they live by. In practice it is a kind of pseudo-country, organisation and identity — a nation defined by what its people believe, not by territory.

It stands for constant expansion, for the right to plain speech, for a long horizon and for knowledge in use. Its rule: love your past, watch your present, plan your future.
